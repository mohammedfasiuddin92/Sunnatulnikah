Privacy & Community Guidelines

Information: We collect account email and the profile information you submit. Approved profile fields may be visible to authenticated members; account email is not included in the match directory.

Purpose: To operate the matrimonial directory, review profiles, and facilitate introductions when requested.

Security: Authentication and access rules are provided by Supabase. No online service is perfectly secure. Do not submit phone numbers, ID documents, addresses, or sensitive personal details in your public profile.

Rights: Contact us to request corrections, profile removal, or account deletion. Additional legal disclosures, retention rules, and regional privacy requirements must be finalized by the site operator before launch.

Conduct: Adults aged 18+ only. Respectful marriage-focused use only. Harassment, impersonation, and financial solicitation are prohibited.

Starter policy — requires review and adaptation for your actual jurisdiction, processes, and data retention practices before accepting real members.
